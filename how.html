How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

  1. 1

    Paste

    Paste a contract or mint address, or a token page link. BluntX detects the chain from the address and the most liquid market.

  2. 2

    Check

    Your browser queries public data sources directly: contract permissions, taxes, liquidity, holders, age and trading activity.

  3. 3

    Read the report

    Each finding adds risk points. The total gives a Low risk, Caution or High risk rating, with every reason listed in plain English.

What we check

The signals that matter, in one report.

Honeypot and taxes

Sell simulations and buy/sell tax on EVM chains and Tron.

Mint permission

Whether new supply can still be created, diluting existing holders.

Freeze authority

Solana tokens whose balances can be frozen, blocking transfers or sales.

Owner permissions

Balance edits, hidden owners, blacklists, pausing, adjustable taxes and unverified code.

Holder concentration

Share held by the top 10 wallets, excluding identified pools and bonding curves.

Liquidity

Pool depth and price impact, LP lock status, and bonding-curve market cap.

Token age

How long the token has traded. Newly launched tokens carry more risk.

Trading activity

24h volume and buy/sell balance, plus additional Solana risk findings.

Methodology

A transparent points score.

The same rules apply on every chain. Each token starts at 0 risk points and each finding adds points. Certain findings result in High risk automatically.

Low risk0–24 pts
Caution25–54 pts
High risk55+ pts
See every rule and its points

Honeypot and taxes

EVM and Tron: sell simulations. A honeypot or a sell tax above 50% = High risk. Sell tax above 10% = +25.

Mint permission

An active Solana mint authority, or an EVM owner with minting rights = +35. Renounced permissions add nothing.

Owner permissions

Owner can modify balances = +40. Hidden owner, reclaimable ownership, unverified source code or a creator linked to previous honeypots = +20–25 each. Blacklist, pause or adjustable tax with an active owner = +15 each.

Freeze authority

Solana: an active freeze authority can prevent holders from transferring or selling. Active = High risk.

Holder concentration

Top 10 wallets, excluding pools and bonding curves: above 50% = +30, above 30% = +15. A single wallet above 15% = +15. Unlocked LP on smaller EVM pools = +15.

Liquidity

Under $5k = +30, under $25k = +15. Tokens still on a pump.fun bonding curve are assessed by market cap: under $10k = +20, under $50k = +10.

Token age

Under 24 hours = +15, under 7 days = +8.

Trading activity

More than 70% of 24h trades are sells, or 24h volume under $1k = +10 each.

Solana risk reports

Public Solana risk reports are included. Flagged as rugged = High risk. Each “danger” finding = +15 (up to 3).

Other chains

For TON, Hyperliquid, Aptos and other chains without a free contract scanner, the rating uses market data only (+10 for the missing contract check) and is labelled accordingly.

Chains

Solana (including pump.fun), Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche and other EVM chains, Tron, Sui, TON and more.
