How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

  1. 1

    Paste

    Paste a contract or mint address, or a token page link. BluntX detects the chain from the address and the most liquid market.

  2. 2

    Check

    Your browser queries public data sources directly: contract permissions, taxes, liquidity, holders, age and trading activity.

  3. 3

    Read the report

    Each finding adds risk points. The total gives a Low risk, Caution or High risk rating, with every reason listed in plain English.

What we check

The signals that matter, in one report.

Honeypot and taxes

Sell simulations and buy, sell and transfer tax on EVM chains and Tron.

Mint and freeze

Whether new supply can be created or balances frozen, including hidden mint paths and Solana Token-2022 controls.

Owner permissions

Balance edits, hidden owners, upgradeable proxies, hidden code, blacklists, pausing, limits and adjustable taxes.

Creator history

Other tokens from the same creator, past rugs or scam links, and how much the creator still holds.

Holders and linked wallets

Top 10 share excluding pools, groups of linked wallets, holders with near-identical balances, shared funding wallets and bundle-style launches.

Liquidity and unlocks

Pool depth, the main pool and its share, LP lock %, when the lock ends, who controls the LP, and upcoming token unlocks.

Age and activity

How long the token has traded, volume, last-hour buy/sell balance and sudden price crashes.

Data confidence

Each report shows High, Medium or Low data confidence and lists anything that could not be checked.

Methodology

A transparent points score.

The same rules apply on every chain. Each token starts at 0 risk points and each finding adds points. Certain findings result in High risk automatically.

Low risk0–24 pts
Caution25–54 pts
High risk55+ pts
See every rule and its points

Honeypot and taxes

EVM and Tron: sell simulations. A honeypot or a sell tax above 50% = High risk. Sell tax above 10% = +25. Solana transfer fee above 10% = +20 (above 5% = +10), plus +5 if the issuer can change it.

Mint permission

An active Solana mint authority, or an EVM owner with minting rights = +35. Renounced permissions add nothing.

Owner permissions

Owner can modify balances = +40 and High risk. Upgradeable proxy with hidden code = High risk. Hidden owner, reclaimable ownership or unverified source code = +20–25 each; hidden mint = +35; upgradeable with an active owner = +20. Blacklist, pause or adjustable tax with an active owner = +15 each; adjustable limits or external calls = +10. Flagged as a copy of another token or an airdrop scam = High risk.

Freeze authority

Solana: an active freeze authority can prevent holders from transferring or selling. Active = High risk. Token-2022 permanent delegate, default-frozen or non-transferable tokens = High risk; transfer hook = +15.

Holder concentration

Top 10 wallets, excluding pools, bonding curves and burn addresses: above 50% = +30, above 30% = +15. A single wallet above 15% = +15.

Liquidity

Under $5k = +30, under $25k = +15. Tokens still on a pump.fun bonding curve are assessed by market cap: under $10k = +20, under $50k = +10.

Token age and activity

Under 24 hours = +15, under 7 days = +8. Heavy selling in the last hour (sells more than twice buys, 50+ trades) = +10; with less activity, more than 70% of 24h trades being sells = +10; 24h volume under $1k = +10. Price down 80% in an hour or 90% in six hours = +25, and High risk if liquidity is also under $1k.

Lookalike names

Another, older token with the same name on the same chain and at least ten times the liquidity = +10, so you can check you have the right address.

Creator history

Creator with past rugs, or a wallet linked to phishing, theft, money laundering or sanctions = High risk. Creator wallet linked to contracts flagged as malicious = +20 (+30 for tokens under 30 days old). 10+ other launches = +15 (3–9 = +8); most of them now nearly worthless = +10. Creator still holds over 20% = +20 (over 5% = +10). Limit 40.

Linked wallets and snipers

Solana, counting only groups of 2 to 50 linked wallets: 30%+ of supply = High risk, 10–30% = +25, 3–10% = +12. One wallet that sent funds or tokens to 3+ current holders with 1%+ of supply = +20 (5+ holders with 3%+, or 10%+ of supply = +30). 5+ of the 20 largest wallets with near-identical balances = +10 (8+ = +20). Automated launch tool = +10. Tokens taken from 5+ holders (or 1% of holders) = High risk. Limit 35.

Liquidity lock and unlocks

Solana main pool under 50% locked = +25 (50–89% = +10); EVM under 90% locked on pools under $500k = +15. Lock ends within 7 days = +20 (30 days = +10). Creator controls half the LP = +25; an ordinary wallet holds half the LP = +20. Paired with an uncommon token = +10. Locked team or vesting tokens (5%+ of supply) unlocking within 7 days = +15 (30 days = +8). Limit 30.

Data confidence

Six areas are marked Checked, Partly checked, Not available or Not applicable. Gaps add few or no points (+5 if the lock is unknown on pools under $100k). Tokens under one hour old are capped at Medium confidence. A Low-confidence report is never shown as Low risk; it shows Caution: not enough data.

Solana risk reports

Public Solana risk reports are included. Flagged as rugged = High risk. Each other “danger” finding = +15 (up to 3).

Other chains

For TON, Hyperliquid, Aptos and other chains without a free contract scanner, the rating uses market data only (+10 for the missing contract check) and is labelled accordingly.

Chains

Solana (including pump.fun), Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche and other EVM chains, Tron, Sui, TON and more.
