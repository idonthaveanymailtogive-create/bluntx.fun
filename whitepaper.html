BluntX Whitepaper

A free, multi-chain token risk checker

Version 1.0 (draft) · November 2026 · bluntx.fun · @BluntXfun

Download PDF

Not financial advice. BluntX scores are automated signals, not guarantees. Always do your own research.

1. The problem

Launching a token now takes minutes and costs almost nothing. That openness is valuable, but it also means a large share of new tokens are built to take money from their buyers. The most common patterns are:

  • Honeypots. The contract lets people buy but blocks or heavily taxes selling, so buyers cannot exit.
  • Rug pulls. The creator removes the liquidity pool, or sells a large hidden allocation, and the price collapses.
  • Owner backdoors. Contract permissions let an owner mint new supply, freeze or blacklist wallets, pause trading, change taxes, or even edit balances.
  • Concentrated supply. A few wallets hold most of the supply and can sell into the market at any time.

Most of this information is public and on-chain. The difficulty is that it is spread across several tools, uses technical language, and varies by chain. Many buyers, especially newer ones, never see it before they buy.

2. What BluntX does

BluntX is a free website that turns that public data into one readable report.

  1. Paste a token's contract or mint address (or a DexScreener / pump.fun link).
  2. BluntX detects the chain, queries several public data sources directly from your browser, and applies a fixed set of rules.
  3. You get a rating of Low risk (Green), Caution (Yellow) or High risk (Red), a numeric risk score, key market statistics, and a list of plain-English findings explaining the rating, with links to DexScreener, block explorers and the underlying reports.

Key properties:

  • Free, no account, no wallet connection. BluntX never asks you to sign anything.
  • Multi-chain. Solana (including pump.fun), Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche and other EVM chains, Tron, Sui, TON and any chain listed on DexScreener.
  • Client-side. The site is static. Checks run in your browser against public APIs; BluntX has no server and stores no data about you or your searches.

Chain detection

The address format gives a first guess: base58 (Solana), 0x + 40 hex characters (EVM), T… (Tron), EQ…/UQ… (TON), 0x…::module::COIN (Sui). BluntX then looks up the address on DexScreener and, in Auto mode, selects the chain with the most liquidity. Users can override the chain with a dropdown. If a token trades on several chains, the report lists the others.

Browser extension (beta)

A Chrome extension brings the same ratings to pump.fun. When switched on for a pump.fun tab, it rates each coin on the page Low risk, Caution or High risk with its risk score, using the same points model as the Solana checker (DexScreener and RugCheck data), and lists the findings in a side panel. It needs no wallet access and makes no transactions. It is available from bluntx.fun as a load-unpacked beta.

3. How scoring works

Every token starts at 0 risk points. Each finding adds points. The total maps to a rating:

Risk score Rating
0–24 Low risk (Green)
25–54 Caution (Yellow)
55 or more High risk (Red)

Some findings make a token High risk automatically, regardless of the total. The rules below are the same ones implemented in the site's code.

Owner-dependent permissions. Some functions only matter if someone can use them. For minting (+35), blacklisting (+15), pausing trading (+15), changing taxes (+15) and per-wallet taxes (+15):

  • if the contract has an active owner, the full points apply;
  • if the owner cannot be determined, half the points apply;
  • if ownership is renounced, no points apply and the report notes the function exists but cannot be used;
  • if GoPlus lists the token as trusted and it has an owner, one third of the points apply (for example, staking emissions on established tokens).

3.4 Holder concentration

BluntX measures the share of supply held by the 10 largest wallets, excluding liquidity pools, bonding curves, locked accounts and burn addresses where they can be identified.

Finding Points
Top 10 wallets hold more than 50% (more than 30%) +30 (+15)
A single wallet holds more than 15% +15
Holder data unavailable +5

Exchange and treasury wallets are counted as holders, so large, established tokens can show elevated concentration. The report notes this.

3.5 Worked examples (live checks, November 2026)

  • PEPE (Ethereum): Low risk, score 15. No honeypot, 0% taxes, ownership renounced, verified code, 99.4% of LP locked or burned; +15 for top-10 concentration of 34.7%.
  • A small pump.fun token (Solana): Caution, score 38. Mint and freeze revoked, but very low market cap (+20), under 7 days old (+8) and very low volume (+10).

5. Limitations

BluntX is a screening tool, not an audit. Please read these limitations before relying on a result.

  • Signals, not guarantees. A Low risk rating means few known risk signals were found at the time of the check. It does not mean a token is safe or a good investment. A High risk rating does not prove fraud.
  • Point-in-time. Owners can change settings, remove liquidity or sell after a check. Results are not monitored.
  • Third-party data. Results depend on external providers, which can be incomplete, delayed, rate-limited or wrong.
  • Unknown techniques. New scam methods may not be detected by any current rule or data source.
  • Holder attribution. Exchange, team, treasury and pool wallets cannot always be told apart.
  • Simulation coverage. Sell simulations are only available on Ethereum, Base and BNB Chain.
  • Limited chains. Chains without a free contract scanner receive a market-data-only rating.
  • Fixed thresholds. The rules are deliberately simple and transparent, which means they will sometimes over- or under-weight a finding for a particular token.

6. Roadmap

Planned features, all intended to remain free. Timelines are not guaranteed and depend on available free infrastructure.

  1. Wallet checker. Paste a wallet address to see which of its holdings carry high-risk findings.
  2. X reply bot. Mention @BluntXfun with a contract address and receive a short summary of the check, with a link to the full report.
  3. Watchlist alerts. Save tokens locally in the browser and get notified when a rating changes, for example if liquidity drops or a permission is re-enabled.
  4. More chains and sources. Contract checks for additional chains as free, browser-compatible scanners become available.
  5. Shareable report cards. A clean image of a report for sharing on social media.
  6. Rule changelog. A public history of every change to the scoring rules.

7. Transparency principles

  • Open methodology. The scoring rules are published on the site and in this document, and match the code.
  • Open code. The site is a static page; anyone can read the code that produces every score.
  • No paid placements. Ratings cannot be bought, and projects cannot pay to change their result.
  • No data collection. No accounts, no wallet connections, no tracking of searched addresses.
  • Neutral language. Findings describe what the data shows and avoid recommendations to buy or sell.
  • Corrections. Errors can be reported publicly via X, and rule changes will be documented.
