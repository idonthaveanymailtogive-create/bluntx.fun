What we check

The same red flags traders already hunt for, gathered into one scan.

  • Developer holdings
  • Top-10 wallet concentration
  • Developer selling activity
  • Honeypot behavior
  • Mint authority
  • Freeze authority
  • Liquidity burned or locked
  • Linked socials

A score you can actually read

Launchguard will rate each coin Safe, Risky or Rug and show why — not just a number with no explanation.

  • Safe

    No major red flags turned up in the checks we run. Still do your own research.

  • Risky

    One or more warnings, such as concentrated holdings or an unlocked mint. Read the reasons first.

  • Rug

    Serious danger signs, such as a honeypot, a selling developer, or pulled liquidity.
