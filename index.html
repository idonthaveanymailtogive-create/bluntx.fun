Launchguard

Scan the coins on a pump.fun tab before you buy.

Launchguard is a browser extension. You have pump.fun open, you turn it on for that tab, and it tells you about every coin on the page — Safe, Risky or Rug, with the reasons.

Built for a pump.fun tab

The official store listing is still coming soon. A Chrome prototype is ready to load unpacked today. This is what it does.

  • On for this tab

    Open pump.fun, flip the Launchguard toggle, and it only runs on that tab.

  • Finds coins on the page

    It reads coin cards and /coin/ links on the tab you already have open — no separate feed to babysit.

  • Safe, Risky or Rug

    Every coin it finds gets a badge and a score, with the reasons spelled out so you can decide for yourself.

  • Safety checks

    Mint and freeze authority, top-holder concentration, liquidity locked or still on the curve, market cap, volume, age and socials.

  • A list of every coin

    An on-page panel and a Chrome side panel list every coin found on the tab, with its score and reasons.

  • Public data, no API key

    The prototype scores from DexScreener and RugCheck’s public APIs. It does not need your wallet or a paid key.

What we check

The prototype uses public DexScreener and RugCheck fields only. If a field is missing, it is skipped, not invented.

  • Mint authority
  • Freeze authority
  • Top-holder concentration
  • RugCheck risk flags
  • Liquidity locked or still on the curve
  • Market cap and 24h volume
  • Pair age
  • Linked socials

A score you can actually read

Each coin is rated Safe, Risky or Rug from those public fields, and the badge and side panel show why — not just a number with no explanation.

  • Safe

    No major red flags turned up in the checks we run. Still do your own research.

  • Risky

    One or more warnings, such as concentrated holdings or an unlocked mint. Read the reasons first.

  • Rug

    Serious danger signs, such as a honeypot, a selling developer, or pulled liquidity.

Load the prototype in Chrome

The official store listing is coming soon. Until then you can load the unpacked extension yourself. It is free, and it is not published to any web store.

  1. 1

    Download the zip

    Get launchguard-extension.zip and unzip it on your computer.

  2. 2

    Load it unpacked

    Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and choose the unzipped folder.

  3. 3

    Turn it on for a pump.fun tab

    Open pump.fun, click the Launchguard icon, and switch it on. Coins on that page get a badge and a list of scores.
