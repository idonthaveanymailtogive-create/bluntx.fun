How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

  1. 1

    Paste

    Paste a contract or mint address, or a DexScreener or pump.fun link. BluntX detects the chain from the address and the most liquid market.

  2. 2

    Check

    Your browser queries public data sources directly: contract permissions, taxes, liquidity, holders, age and trading activity.

  3. 3

    Read the report

    Each finding adds risk points. The total gives a Low risk, Caution or High risk rating, with every reason listed in plain English.

What we check

The signals that matter, in one report.

Honeypot and taxes

Sell simulations and buy/sell tax on EVM chains and Tron, via GoPlus and honeypot.is.

Mint permission

Whether new supply can still be created, diluting existing holders.

Freeze authority

Solana tokens whose balances can be frozen, blocking transfers or sales.

Owner permissions

Balance edits, hidden owners, blacklists, pausing, adjustable taxes and unverified code.

Holder concentration

Share held by the top 10 wallets, excluding identified pools and bonding curves.

Liquidity

Pool depth and price impact, LP lock status, and bonding-curve market cap.

Token age

How long the token has traded. Newly launched tokens carry more risk.

Trading activity

24h volume and buy/sell balance, plus RugCheck findings on Solana.

Methodology

A transparent points score.

The same rules apply on every chain. Each token starts at 0 risk points and each finding adds points. Certain findings result in High risk automatically.

Low risk0–24 pts
Caution25–54 pts
High risk55+ pts
See every rule and its points

Honeypot and taxes

EVM and Tron: GoPlus Security and honeypot.is sell simulations (Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain). A honeypot or a sell tax above 50% = High risk. Sell tax above 10% = +25.

Mint permission

An active Solana mint authority, or an EVM owner with minting rights = +35. Renounced permissions add nothing.

Owner permissions

Owner can modify balances = +40. Hidden owner, reclaimable ownership, unverified source code or a creator linked to previous honeypots = +20–25 each. Blacklist, pause or adjustable tax with an active owner = +15 each.

Freeze authority

Solana: an active freeze authority can prevent holders from transferring or selling. Active = High risk.

Holder concentration

Top 10 wallets, excluding pools and bonding curves: above 50% = +30, above 30% = +15. A single wallet above 15% = +15. Unlocked LP on smaller EVM pools = +15.

Liquidity

Under $5k = +30, under $25k = +15. Tokens still on a pump.fun bonding curve are assessed by market cap: under $10k = +20, under $50k = +10.

Token age

Under 24 hours = +15, under 7 days = +8.

Trading activity

More than 70% of 24h trades are sells, or 24h volume under $1k = +10 each.

RugCheck (Solana)

The public RugCheck report is included. Flagged as rugged = High risk. Each “danger” finding = +15 (up to 3).

Other chains

For TON, Hyperliquid, Aptos and other chains without a free contract scanner, the rating uses market data only (+10 for the missing contract check) and is labelled accordingly.

Data sources

DexScreener (all chains), GoPlus Security (EVM, Tron, Sui), honeypot.is (Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain), Solana RPC and RugCheck (Solana). All checks run live in your browser.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is BluntX free?

Yes. The checker and the browser extension are free to use. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. BluntX never asks for a wallet connection, a signature or a private key. If anything claiming to be BluntX asks for one, it is not us.

What data do you store?

None. The site is static. Checks run in your browser against public APIs, and BluntX has no server that sees your searches.

Does Low risk mean a token is safe?

No. Low risk means few known risk signals were found at the time of the check. Owners can change settings or remove liquidity later, and new techniques may not be detected. It is not financial advice.

Which chains are supported?

Full contract checks on Solana (including pump.fun), Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum and other EVM chains, Tron and Sui. Other chains listed on DexScreener get a market-data-only rating, clearly labelled.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. On phone or tablet, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” option to get BluntX with its own icon.

Where does the data come from?

DexScreener, GoPlus Security, honeypot.is, Solana public RPC and RugCheck. The report lists which sources answered and links to each one.
