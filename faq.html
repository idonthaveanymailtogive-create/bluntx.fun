FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is BluntX free?

Yes. The checker and the browser extension are free to use. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. BluntX never asks for a wallet connection, a signature or a private key. If anything claiming to be BluntX asks for one, it is not us.

What data do you store?

None. The site is static. Checks run in your browser against public APIs, and BluntX has no server that sees your searches.

Does Low risk mean a token is safe?

No. Low risk means few known risk signals were found at the time of the check. Owners can change settings or remove liquidity later, and new techniques may not be detected. It is not financial advice.

Which chains are supported?

Full contract checks on Solana (including pump.fun), Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum and other EVM chains, Tron and Sui. Other supported chains get a market-data-only rating, clearly labelled.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. On phone or tablet, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” option to get BluntX with its own icon.

Where does the data come from?

Data from public sources including DexScreener, RugCheck, GoPlus and Honeypot.is. BluntX is independent and not affiliated with them. Each report lists which sources answered.
